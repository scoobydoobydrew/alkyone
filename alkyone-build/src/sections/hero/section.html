Concept Inside the flexible circuit

Drawing of the concept. Dashed parts are exploratory. Not to scale.

  1. Monitoring

    The patch notices the pulse and the oxygen in the blood with an sensor, skin temperature, motion and position, sound, and whether it is on the skin.

  2. Processing on the device

    Sound is itself, and no audio is stored.

  3. Machine learning

    Learning what is typical for this baby, not babies in general. An idea we want to explore. It is not built.

  4. Predictive maintenance

    Warn about a weak battery, poor skin contact or a fading signal before they cause trouble. An idea we want to explore. It is not built.

  5. The link

    The patch sends what it notices to the ring over , with a steady .

  6. Fallback routes

    If the heartbeat stops, silence is treated as an alert. The alert repeats until someone acknowledges it, and the app is told too. Passing it to a second person is an idea we want to explore.
