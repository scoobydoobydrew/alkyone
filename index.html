1 The system · concept

One loop that closes.

Alkyone is a concept for a single closed loop: notice, send, alert, acknowledge, learn. Every step has a fallback, so a quiet moment is never just assumed to be fine.

Pick a step, or try breaking one

Tap a step on the loop to read what it does and what happens if it fails.

The buzz scales with the need

  • Routine One soft buzz. Nothing to do.
  • Needs attention A firm pattern that repeats until you acknowledge it.
  • No answer It keeps repeating. Passing it to a second person is an idea we want to explore.

2 The patch

A small patch that notices.

It sits on the chest. Tap a dot to see what each part does.

  • Light shines on the skin and an sensor reads the pulse and the oxygen in the blood.

  • A small sensor reads the temperature of the skin under the patch.

  • An feels movement and tilt, so the patch knows when the baby is still, moving or has turned.

  • Listens for sound. The sound is itself, and no audio is stored.

  • Knows whether the patch is touching skin. If it comes off, that is something to tell you about, not a reason to go quiet.

5 Sleep position · exploratory

A simulated night, replayed.

Drag the timeline. The figure turns as the patch tilts.

One sensor. One angle. Three postures.

One sensor on the chest can tell how the chest is tilted. It cannot see arms, legs or head.

It shows position. It does not diagnose or judge.
